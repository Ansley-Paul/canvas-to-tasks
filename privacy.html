Privacy Policy

Canvas to Google Tasks is a personal-use application that synchronizes assignment and quiz information from Canvas with Google Tasks.

Information accessed

The application accesses Canvas course, assignment, quiz, title, and due-date information in order to create and update Google Tasks.

The application also accesses the user's Google Tasks account so that it can create, update, and manage tasks requested by the user.

How information is used

Information is used solely to provide the synchronization functionality. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Data sharing

The application does not sell or share user information with third parties.

Data storage

Synchronization information is stored locally on the user's computer. Google OAuth credentials are also stored locally and are not intentionally transmitted to the application developer.
